Esteban Garnica Sánchez

Art as a Tool for Social Transformation

Esteban Garnica Sánchez (Santiago, 1985) is a Chilean visual artist trained at the University of Chile. His career combines artistic creation, teaching, and urban space intervention. His work focuses on current issues such as social inequality, environmental pollution, and urban conflicts, using techniques that range from mural painting to public space interventions.

Garnica describes himself as a chronicler of his time. His projects, which include large-scale murals and street actions, aim to transform the perception of public space and foster civic debate.

Furthermore, his educational work, carried out through workshops and collaborative projects, contributes to shaping new generations of artists committed to social change.
